# Family-organiser apps: what they offer beyond a plain todo list

Researched 2026-10-25 from vendor-owned pages only (cozi.com, support.familywall.com / familywall.com,
getbring.com help centre and feature pages, help.anylist.com / anylist.com, ourhomeapp.com, picniic.com).

**Method caveat.** The session's network policy refused direct HTTPS to every vendor host
(www.cozi.com, support.familywall.com, getbring.com, help.anylist.com all answered 403 on CONNECT), so pages
could not be fetched verbatim. Every claim below comes from a vendor-domain-restricted web-search index of the
cited page (quotes are the index's extracts of the vendor text). "Silent" means the indexed vendor page(s) did not
state it. Where a vendor page is a blog post rather than a help article this is noted; Cozi keeps most of its
how-to content on cozi.com blog/help pages rather than a separate Zendesk.

## Cozi Family Organizer

### 1. Lists
- Two list kinds: shopping lists and to-do lists; "add as many personal & shared to-do lists as you want" ([Cozi to-do lists](https://www.cozi.com/to-do-lists/)).
- A to-do list has a single owner or is marked "Shared"; owner can be changed when editing the list ([Getting started with to-do lists](https://www.cozi.com/getting-started-with-cozi-to-do-lists/), [Improvements to Cozi lists](https://www.cozi.com/blog/improvements-to-cozi-lists/)).
- Colour-coding: per-member colour is a calendar concept; lists are not colour-coded by member (silent on any list colouring) ([Cozi calendar](https://www.cozi.com/calendar/)).
- Templates: an in-app "List Library" of premade checklists (Birthday Party Checklist, Vacation Planning, After School Checklist, weekly grocery template) which you add, then rename/edit ([List Library](https://www.cozi.com/list-library/), [Grocery list template](https://www.cozi.com/add-to-cozi/grocery-list-template/)).
- Recurring "staples": no recurring-list feature; the vendor's suggested pattern is a separate master list of frequently bought items reset with "Uncheck all" ([Getting started with shopping lists](https://www.cozi.com/getting-started-with-cozi-shopping-lists/)).
- Section headings inside a list: type an item in ALL CAPS (e.g. PRODUCE) to create a heading; in the apps tap "+" in a heading bar to add items under it ([How to add headings](https://www.cozi.com/blog/how-add-headings-cozi-list/), [Organize lists with section headings](https://www.cozi.com/blog/organize-your-cozi-lists/)).

### 2. Items
- An item is a free-text line added via "Add New Item" + Enter ([Getting started with shopping lists](https://www.cozi.com/getting-started-with-cozi-shopping-lists/)). Dedicated quantity, notes, or category fields: silent (categories are the ALL-CAPS headings above).
- Assigning items to a member: shopping/to-do items are not assigned; the whole list has an owner. Chore items live in per-member chore lists (see 6).
- Due dates on to-dos: a 2011 blog says typing a numeric date into a to-do adds it to the calendar, and such dated items cannot carry reminders ([Add to-do tasks to calendar, 2011](https://blogs.cozi.com/coziblog/2011/04/add-to-do-list-tasks-to-your-calendar.html)); current help pages are silent, so treat as unsupported/unverified.

### 3. Family model
- One shared account for up to 12 members; each member gets a name and a colour; colours changeable in Settings ([FAQ](https://www.cozi.com/faq/), [Cozi calendar](https://www.cozi.com/calendar/)).
- Members sign in with their own email address plus the one shared account password ([FAQ](https://www.cozi.com/faq/), [Email addresses in Cozi accounts](https://www.cozi.com/help-mobile-email-addresses/)).
- A member can be added without an email address (gets a colour dot on the calendar) but then cannot sign in, receive email reminders or agenda emails ([How to add a family member](https://www.cozi.com/blog/how-to-add-family-to-cozi/)).
- Kids: added like any member, with an email so they can sign in with the family password ([Get your kids on Cozi](https://www.cozi.com/blog/get-your-kids-on-cozi/)).
- Permissions: "fully shared account"; every member with an email can view, add, edit and delete all data; no restricted-access option ([FAQ](https://www.cozi.com/faq/)).
- Per-member notifications: reminders go only to event attendees, by push or email; daily/weekly agenda emails are enabled per member in Settings ([Get Cozi to remind your family](https://www.cozi.com/blog/get-cozi-to-remind-your-family-for-you/), [Daily agenda emails](https://www.cozi.com/blog/daily-agenda-emails/)).

### 4. Calendar
- Shared calendar; events show in the attendee's colour; filter to one person or all ([Cozi calendar](https://www.cozi.com/calendar/)).
- Recurring events via "Repeats" (e.g. Every Week On Mon–Fri) ([Create a daily schedule](https://www.cozi.com/blog/how-to-create-a-daily-schedule-using-cozi/)).
- Agenda emails: daily (morning) and weekly (Sunday), email-only, per member, require verified email ([Daily agenda emails](https://www.cozi.com/blog/daily-agenda-emails/), [Be prepared with agenda emails](https://www.cozi.com/blog/be-prepared-with-agenda-emails/)).
- "Cozi Today" view: upcoming appointments, current to-dos and recently added shopping items for the day ([Cozi calendar](https://www.cozi.com/calendar/)).
- Meals land on the calendar: a scheduled meal is placed on a date with a mealtime of breakfast, lunch, snack or dinner, and "Meals you create from the Meal Planner will display on your calendar" ([Meals and recipe box](https://www.cozi.com/meals-and-recipe-box/), [Cozi meal planner](https://www.cozi.com/blog/cozi-meal-planner/)).
- Gold-only: mobile month view, calendar search, up to 3 reminders per attendee (free: 1), "change notifications" sent to attendees other than the editor when an event changes ([Cozi Gold features](https://www.cozi.com/cozi-gold-features/), [Cozi Gold](https://www.cozi.com/cozi-gold/)).

### 5. Meal planning
- Recipe box holds recipes; from a recipe tap Add to Calendar (iOS) / three-dot menu (Android), pick date + mealtime; web support is described inconsistently across vendor pages ([Meal plan with Cozi](https://www.cozi.com/blog/meal-plan-with-cozi/), [Getting started with Cozi Meals](https://www.cozi.com/getting-started-with-cozi-recipe-box/)).
- Meal Planner highlights days with busy schedules so quicker meals can be planned there ([Meals and recipe box](https://www.cozi.com/meals-and-recipe-box/)).
- Add ingredients to list: tap the shopping-cart icon on a recipe, choose an existing list or create one; ingredients are nested under the recipe name as a heading ([Meals and recipe box](https://www.cozi.com/meals-and-recipe-box/), [Meal planning from the mobile app](https://www.cozi.com/blog/meal-planning-from-the-cozi-mobile-app-2/)).
- Removing a meal and its effect on the shopping list: silent.
- Paid "Cozi Max" tier adds an AI meal planner ([Introducing Cozi Max](https://www.cozi.com/blog/introducing-cozi-max/)).

### 6. Chores / kids
- "Cozi Chores": every family member automatically gets a chores list; a chore's pencil icon sets daily or weekly repeat; weekly chores reappear on the weekday created; a pin icon beside a member's name pins that list to the top for your account; app-only ([Introducing Cozi Chores](https://www.cozi.com/blog/cozi-chores/)).
- Rewards/points: silent (none described). Kid-facing mode: none; kids use the same app with full access ([FAQ](https://www.cozi.com/faq/)).

### 7. Reminders and notifications
- Event reminders by push or email, attendees only, 1 per attendee (free) / 3 (Gold) ([Get Cozi to remind your family](https://www.cozi.com/blog/get-cozi-to-remind-your-family-for-you/)).
- Push for list changes: silent (Shopping Mode, Gold, highlights items others added while you shop) ([Introducing Shopping Mode](https://www.cozi.com/blog/introducing-shopping-mode-cozi-gold/)).
- Gold change notifications for edited events, default recipients = attendees minus the editor, editable ([Cozi Gold features](https://www.cozi.com/cozi-gold-features/)).

### 8. Completion and clearing
- Checking an item adds a check mark and strike-through; it stays on the list ([Getting started with to-do lists](https://www.cozi.com/getting-started-with-cozi-to-do-lists/)).
- Bulk actions: Check all, Uncheck all, Delete checked (apps: menu beside "+"; web: Remove menu); single-item trash icon on web; "Check all" + "Delete all" also deletes headings ([Getting started with shopping lists](https://www.cozi.com/getting-started-with-cozi-shopping-lists/)).
- Recurring items: only via chores (daily/weekly) ([Introducing Cozi Chores](https://www.cozi.com/blog/cozi-chores/)).

### 9. Offline / sync / attribution
- Android offline mode shows last-downloaded data for features already viewed; offline changes queue and sync on reconnect; for calendar conflicts "the first change sent ... will be saved and any subsequent changes made offline after that first change will be ignored" ([Android offline mode](https://www.cozi.com/help-mobile-android-offline-cached-en_gb/)).
- Shopping lists: check off, add, rearrange offline; sync on reconnect ([Shopping lists](https://www.cozi.com/shopping-lists/)).
- Attribution of who changed what: silent (only the Gold change-notification excludes "the person who's identified as making the change") ([Cozi Gold features](https://www.cozi.com/cozi-gold-features/)).

### 10. Distinctive
- Single household account with per-member colour and email-based identity; meals as a first-class calendar entry with breakfast/lunch/snack/dinner slots; meal planner flags busy days; ALL-CAPS headings as a zero-UI grouping trick; daily/weekly agenda emails; chore lists auto-created per member with daily/weekly repeat.

## FamilyWall

### 1. Lists
- Shopping lists and to-do lists (plus wish lists, chore checklists); to-do lists can be private or shared ([About FamilyWall](https://support.familywall.com/en/support/solutions/articles/47001013681-about-familywall)).
- Shopping lists are shared to the circle; private shopping lists: silent ([About FamilyWall](https://support.familywall.com/en/support/solutions/articles/47001013681-about-familywall)).
- List templates: silent. Recurring tasks (daily, weekly, custom) exist but are Premium-only ([Task Quick Add](https://support.familywall.com/en/support/solutions/articles/47001271445-use-task-quick-add-in-list)).
- Colour per member applies to calendar events, not lists ([Default colour per member](https://support.familywall.com/en/support/solutions/articles/47001246093-how-do-i-change-the-default-color-per-member-)).

### 2. Items
- Quick Add fields: category (e.g. Work, Home, Fruits & Vegetables, Dairy), assignee, due date with optional time ("Tomorrow at 4 PM"), reminder before due, recurrence (Premium); suggestions pre-filled from popular items and your own history ([Task Quick Add](https://support.familywall.com/en/support/solutions/articles/47001271445-use-task-quick-add-in-list)).
- Due date + reminder also settable later from a task's options menu, app and web ([Due date and reminder on a todo](https://support.familywall.com/en/support/solutions/articles/47001245224-how-to-add-a-due-date-and-a-reminder-to-a-todo-)).
- Quantity field on shopping items: silent. Notes: silent.

### 3. Family model
- A "Family" is a circle; founder is administrator by default; admins can edit/delete anyone's content and invite/remove members; simple members can only change/delete what they published ([Manage administration rights](https://support.familywall.com/en/support/solutions/articles/47001239797-how-to-manage-member-administration-rights-)).
- Invite by email ([How can I invite members](https://support.familywall.com/en/support/solutions/articles/47001013699-how-can-i-invite-members-)); "Create a Child account" for members without email: admin sets username and password, credentials are emailed to the admin to hand over; usable for any email-less member; such accounts are simple members, not admins ([Invite a member without email](https://support.familywall.com/en/support/solutions/articles/47001239550-invite-a-member-without-email-child-account-)).
- Additional circles (extended family, friends) each with own content and members; Premium limited to 5 circles ([Add a new circle](https://support.familywall.com/en/support/solutions/articles/47001252124-add-a-new-circle), [5-circle limit](https://support.familywall.com/en/support/solutions/articles/47001268851-how-many-circles-can-i-have-with-a-familywall-premium-subscription-)).
- Default colour per member set in My Family > member > Edit; applies to that member's events unless overridden per event ([Default colour per member](https://support.familywall.com/en/support/solutions/articles/47001246093-how-do-i-change-the-default-color-per-member-)).
- Notifications: global toggle plus per-section toggles ([Deactivate notifications](https://support.familywall.com/en/support/solutions/articles/47001013707-how-can-i-deactivate-notifications-)).

### 4. Calendar
- Shared, colour-coded by member; view one person or everyone ([Premium contents](https://support.familywall.com/en/support/solutions/articles/47001239196-what-is-included-in-familywall-premium-)).
- Overlays switchable in calendar settings, each with its own colour: Scheduled tasks (to-dos with due dates), Meals, Timetables, Public holidays ([Scheduled tasks in calendar](https://support.familywall.com/en/support/solutions/articles/47001241417-how-to-display-scheduled-tasks-in-the-calendar-), [Meals in calendar](https://support.familywall.com/en/support/solutions/articles/47001239539-how-to-display-my-familywall-meals-in-the-calendar-), [Timetables in calendar](https://support.familywall.com/en/support/solutions/articles/47001239508-how-to-display-timetables-in-the-calendar-), [Holiday colour](https://support.familywall.com/en/support/solutions/articles/47001252065-how-do-i-change-the-default-color-of-the-public-holidays)).
- "Daily Briefing Notification": calendar setting, default 9am, lists all of the day's appointments ([Daily reminder](https://support.familywall.com/en/support/solutions/articles/47001250678-set-a-daily-reminder-for-the-day-s-appointments)).
- Recurring events: silent in indexed help. Google/Outlook sync and subscribe-by-URL are Premium ([About FamilyWall](https://support.familywall.com/en/support/solutions/articles/47001013681-about-familywall)).

### 5. Meal planning
- Meal Planner (Premium) with Recipe Box; meal types (breakfast/lunch/dinner...) are editable: rename, change emoji or hours, add new; hide/delete only on web ([Hide or delete meal types](https://support.familywall.com/en/support/solutions/articles/47001239799-how-to-hide-or-delete-meal-types-)).
- Recipes get categories and the same category filter bar appears in the planner ([Classify recipes](https://support.familywall.com/en/support/solutions/articles/47001240194-how-to-classify-recipes-)); drag-and-drop recipes onto days, import from the web ([familywall.com](https://www.familywall.com/en/index.html)).
- "Create shopping lists based on your meal plan" / "add missing ingredients to the shopping list" ([Premium contents](https://support.familywall.com/en/support/solutions/articles/47001239196-what-is-included-in-familywall-premium-), [Home Hub](https://www.familywall.com/homehub.html)). Exact steps and what happens on meal removal: silent.
- First day of week for the planner is configurable ([First day of week](https://support.familywall.com/en/support/solutions/articles/47001239897-change-the-first-day-of-the-week-for-the-meal-planner)).

### 6. Chores / kids
- "Chore checklist for kids" is a to-do list with assignees ([About FamilyWall](https://support.familywall.com/en/support/solutions/articles/47001013681-about-familywall)). Rewards/points: silent. Child accounts (see 3) are the kid-facing provision; "Timetables" (Premium) hold recurring or alternating weekly class/activity schedules ([Premium contents](https://support.familywall.com/en/support/solutions/articles/47001239196-what-is-included-in-familywall-premium-)).

### 7. Reminders and notifications
- Task reminder before due date; daily briefing at a chosen hour; per-section notification toggles ([Due date and reminder](https://support.familywall.com/en/support/solutions/articles/47001245224-how-to-add-a-due-date-and-a-reminder-to-a-todo-), [Daily reminder](https://support.familywall.com/en/support/solutions/articles/47001250678-set-a-daily-reminder-for-the-day-s-appointments), [Deactivate notifications](https://support.familywall.com/en/support/solutions/articles/47001013707-how-can-i-deactivate-notifications-)). Push on list changes specifically: silent.

### 8. Completion and clearing
- Items are checked while shopping; "lists are automatically updated as they get completed" ([familywall.com](https://www.familywall.com/en/index.html)). Strike-through vs removal, bulk clear: silent.

### 9. Offline / sync / attribution
- Lists viewable and checkable offline at the store; items added by others are visible ([About FamilyWall](https://support.familywall.com/en/support/solutions/articles/47001013681-about-familywall)). Who-added/who-completed history: silent. Permission model is per-author (simple members edit only own content) ([Manage administration rights](https://support.familywall.com/en/support/solutions/articles/47001239797-how-to-manage-member-administration-rights-)).

### 10. Distinctive
- Admin-created child accounts (username/password, no email); author-scoped edit rights for non-admins; calendar overlays (tasks, meals, timetables) each toggleable with its own colour; customisable meal types with hours; Quick Add parser with category, assignee, date/time, reminder, recurrence in one entry; wall-mounted "Home Hub" tablet as the shared household display ([Home Hub](https://www.familywall.com/homehub.html)).

## Bring! Shopping List

### 1. Lists
- Multiple lists; create from the list menu; at least two lists must exist to delete one; list order draggable in the overview or via Profile > "Sort List Overview" ([Items & Lists](https://www.getbring.com/help-center-main-categories/items-lists), [Profile and settings](https://www.getbring.com/help-center-main-categories/profile-settings)).
- Sharing per list: long-press > "Invite Friends"; remove via "Manage participants" ([Items & Lists](https://www.getbring.com/help-center-main-categories/items-lists)). Per-member colour: silent.
- Templates: in Inspiration tap "+" to create a list template or recipe template with a name, theme, items, optional items and an optional recipe link; share a template via "..." > link, which opens in the recipient's app ([Inspirations & suggestions](https://www.getbring.com/help-center-main-categories/inspirations-suggestions)). This is the "weekly staples" mechanism: a template re-applied to the list.

### 2. Items
- Items come from a catalogue of common purchases with icons; unusual items are added as "Own Items" ([Items & Lists](https://www.getbring.com/help-center-main-categories/items-lists)).
- Quantity/description/photo via item details, or inline in the search bar ("2 kg apples"); details are stored per list, not globally; catalogue items cannot be moved between lists, but items can be moved from the list, Recently Used or Own Items ([Items & Lists](https://www.getbring.com/help-center-main-categories/items-lists)).
- Labels on an item: "urgent", "if convenient", "on offer" ([New labels](https://www.getbring.com/blog-posts/new-labels-for-more-efficient-grocery-planning)).
- Categories (sections) are reorderable under List settings and hideable with the eye icon; items sort by category order, never individually ([Profile and settings](https://www.getbring.com/help-center-main-categories/profile-settings)). Assigning an item to a person: silent.

### 3. Family model
- Each participant has their own Bring! account/profile; lists are shared to participants by invitation; no roles described ([Items & Lists](https://www.getbring.com/help-center-main-categories/items-lists)). Kid accounts, colours, who-sees-what: silent beyond list membership.

### 4. Calendar
- None. The vendor's meal-planning guidance is to use templates/recipes per weekday, not a calendar ([Weekly meal planning with Bring!](https://www.getbring.com/blog-posts/meal-planning-for-the-week-with-bring)).

### 5. Recipes / inspiration
- Inspiration tab streams recipes from recipe platforms and food blogs; save with the heart button into "My collection"; import your own recipes via the browser share sheet or the Bring! recipe import button (only recipes in the correct structured format) ([Inspirations & suggestions](https://www.getbring.com/help-center-main-categories/inspirations-suggestions), [Inspired feature page](https://www.getbring.com/en/features/inspired)).
- Recipe-to-list: recipes carry an integrated shopping list; ingredients are ticked/added to the chosen list ([Inspired](https://www.getbring.com/en/features/inspired)).
- "Shopping suggestions": based on items you have already bought with Bring!; computed only for the 400 most-used catalogue items, so own items get none ([Inspirations & suggestions](https://www.getbring.com/help-center-main-categories/inspirations-suggestions)). Also pairing suggestions when adding an item (spaghetti -> tomato sauce) ([Best shopping list app blog](https://www.getbring.com/blog-posts/the-best-shopping-list-app)).
- Offers: an Offers tab (CH, DE, AT, FR only) shows retailer brochures by location; brochure items can be added to the list; "Matching Offers" alerts for list items on sale at preferred stores ([Items & Lists](https://www.getbring.com/help-center-main-categories/items-lists), [How Bring helps you save money](https://getbring.com/blog-posts/how-bring-helps-you-save-money)). Loyalty cards storable in-app ([Loyalty cards](https://www.getbring.com/blog-posts/save-your-loyalty-cards-in-bring)).

### 6. Chores / kids
- None.

### 7. Reminders and notifications
- List participants are notified when a shared list changes or when someone goes shopping; requires accepted invitation plus notification permission under Profile > More Settings > Permissions ([Items & Lists](https://www.getbring.com/help-center-main-categories/items-lists), [Best shopping list app blog](https://www.getbring.com/blog-posts/the-best-shopping-list-app)). Due dates: none.

### 8. Completion and clearing
- Tapping an item removes it from the list into "Recently Used" (last few items), from which a tap re-adds it; no strike-through state ([Items & Lists](https://www.getbring.com/help-center-main-categories/items-lists)).

### 9. Offline / sync / attribution
- "Activities" section shows who added or bought each item over the last days/weeks, allows re-adding from history, emoji reactions and quick messages to list members ([Your activities at a glance](https://www.getbring.com/blog-posts/your-activities-at-a-glance)). Offline behaviour: silent. Works across phone, watch, web and voice assistants ([Anywhere, anytime](https://www.getbring.com/en/features/anywhere-anytime), [Voice assistants](https://www.getbring.com/help-center-main-categories/voice-assistants)).

### 10. Distinctive
- Catalogue-with-icons plus Own Items; purchase = removal into Recently Used (fast re-add); templates as reusable lists; per-list item details; urgency labels; purchase-history-driven suggestions; activity log with attribution; "someone is shopping" notification.

## Patterns worth copying for two parents + school-age kids

1. Household = one tenant; members have a name and a colour; identity by email with a shared household secret (Cozi) or admin-minted username/password for kids (FamilyWall).
2. Meals are calendar entries with a slot (breakfast/lunch/snack/dinner, slots editable with hours); the day view shows events + meals + due tasks together; busy days flagged for quick meals.
3. Recipe -> ingredients -> chosen list, ingredients grouped under a recipe heading (Cozi) or merged with summed quantities and de-duplicated against what is already listed (AnyList).
4. Two completion models: strike-through + "Delete checked" (Cozi/AnyList) vs remove-into-Recently-Used with one-tap re-add (Bring!). Keep a recent/favourites pool either way.
5. Templates (Bring!, Cozi List Library, AnyList meal-plan templates) cover "weekly staples" without true recurrence; recurrence belongs on chores/tasks (daily/weekly, Premium in FamilyWall).
6. Chores: auto list per member, daily/weekly repeat, optional approval and points (OurHome); pin a kid's list to the top.
7. Notifications: reminders only to attendees/assignees; daily briefing at a set hour; "list changed" and "someone is shopping" pushes; change notices exclude the editor.
8. Attribution: Bring!'s Activities log (who added/bought) is the only explicit audit trail; FamilyWall enforces author-only edits for non-admins.
9. Offline: last-write-wins with later offline edits dropped (Cozi) is the documented floor; list check-offs queue and sync.
10. Suggestions from purchase history, per-store aisle ordering, urgency labels and quantity-as-text ("2 kg apples") are cheap list ergonomics.
