# Apple Reminders, Google Tasks, Any.do — feature inventory from vendor documentation

Researched 2026-10-25. **Method note:** direct page fetches to support.apple.com, support.google.com, developers.google.com and support.any.do were refused by the session's egress proxy (policy 403), so every claim below comes from search-engine excerpts of the cited vendor pages retrieved today. Where an excerpt did not cover a topic the entry says "silent". No third-party sources are used for claims; vendor blogs are marked as such.

## Apple Reminders (iOS 17/18 iPhone User Guide + Apple Support articles)

### 1. Structure
- Lists: created with Add List, with name, colour and symbol; "List Type" offers Standard, Groceries or Smart List. Icons only available for lists stored in iCloud. [Make a grocery list](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/make-a-grocery-list-iph80ba26e1f/ios), [Edit and organize a list](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/edit-and-organize-a-list-iph82596cb20/ios)
- Sections: More > New Section; sections collapse/expand; an existing list can be viewed as columns (More > View as Columns) and items dragged between columns to recategorise. With Apple Intelligence, "Auto-Categorize" sorts related reminders into sections automatically; turning it off lets you keep or remove the sections without deleting reminders. [Edit and organize a list](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/edit-and-organize-a-list-iph82596cb20/ios)
- Sub-tasks: Edit Details > Subtasks; subtasks require iCloud Reminders. Deleting a reminder deletes its subtasks. [Use Reminders (102484)](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102484), [iCloud guide](https://support.apple.com/en-ie/guide/icloud/mmc0cd77d9/icloud)
- Groups of lists: More > Edit Lists > Add Group, name it, pick lists; or drag one list onto another. [Organize multiple lists](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/organize-multiple-lists-iph2c6cf708e/ios), [Organize reminders (119953)](https://support.apple.com/en-us/119953)
- Templates: More > Save as Template, optionally including completed reminders; later edits to list or template do not affect each other; More > Templates creates a list from one. [Work with templates](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/work-with-templates-iph3735c6147/ios)
- Pinned lists: touch-and-hold > Pin or swipe right; maximum nine pinned lists. [Organize reminders (119953)](https://support.apple.com/en-us/119953)

### 2. Item fields
- Title, notes, URL, priority, tags, list, notification settings via Edit Details. [102484](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102484)
- Date and Time buttons in the quick toolbar above the keyboard; a date without time notifies at 9:00 AM by default; reminders use current-location time zone (iOS 26 adds a fixed time zone option). [Create reminders](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/create-reminders-iph88463e18/ios), [102484](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102484)
- Location reminder: Location button; requires precise location permission. [Create reminders](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/create-reminders-iph88463e18/ios)
- "When Messaging": Edit Details > turn on When Messaging > choose contact; notification appears next time you chat with that person in Messages. [102484](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102484)
- Repeat: turn on Date or Time, tap Repeat, choose a frequency. Custom repeat grammar: silent in the iPhone excerpts (Mac guide reference exists but not quoted here). [Add details](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/add-details-iphec7a1de82/ios)
- Early Reminder: lead time such as "5 minutes before" or "2 days before"; Urgent: alarm fires even when silenced / in a Focus. [Add details](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/add-details-iphec7a1de82/ios)
- Priority displayed as one/two/three exclamation marks (low/medium/high per Mac guide; iCloud.com shows the marks). [iCloud guide](https://support.apple.com/en-ie/guide/icloud/mmc0cd77d9/icloud)
- Tags: one word each (dashes/underscores allowed), add via Tag button or typing `#name`; multiple tags per item; bulk-tag via Select. [Tag items](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/tag-items-iph474a6685c/ios)
- Flag: Flag button in quick toolbar. Attachments: photo or scanned document from the quick toolbar. [Create reminders](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/create-reminders-iph88463e18/ios)
- Assignee: only on shared lists (see §6).

### 3. Capture
- New Reminder button; Control Center "Reminders" control creates a reminder without switching apps. [Create reminders](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/create-reminders-iph88463e18/ios)
- Natural language: with Apple Intelligence, typing "Feed the cat every evening at 5pm" fills date, time and daily repeat. Without Apple Intelligence: silent. [Create reminders](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/create-reminders-iph88463e18/ios)
- Siri: "Remind me tomorrow at 3:00 p.m. to call Tara", "Remind me to check the mail when I get home", "Add artichokes to my groceries list". [102484](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102484), [Make a grocery list](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/make-a-grocery-list-iph80ba26e1f/ios)
- Share sheet: Share button in another app > Reminders adds a link back to the source (website, map location). With Apple Intelligence, sharing a Safari recipe or Notes text produces a list of suggested grocery items with a list picker. [Create reminders](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/create-reminders-iph88463e18/ios), [Make a grocery list](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/make-a-grocery-list-iph80ba26e1f/ios)
- Email-to-task: silent. Autocomplete from history: silent.

### 4. Smart lists
- Built-in: Today (today + overdue), Scheduled (anything with date/time), Flagged, Assigned to Me (items assigned to you in shared lists); All and Completed also exist; toggled under Edit Lists. [Use Smart Lists](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/use-smart-lists-iphe882772ed/ios)
- Custom: new list > List Type Smart List > Edit Filters; filters are tags, dates, times, locations, flags, priority; match "any" or "all"; reminders stay in their original lists. [Use Smart Lists](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/use-smart-lists-iphe882772ed/ios)
- Tag browser below lists filters by one or more tags (all selected tags must match); view can be saved as a Smart List. [Tag items](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/tag-items-iph474a6685c/ios)

### 5. Completion
- Tap the circle; completed items are hidden by default; More > Show Completed reveals them; Clear deletes completed items. [Complete items](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/complete-items-iph3fb74d597/ios)
- Repeating: completing an instance moves it to completed and the next occurrence appears (stated in Mac guide; iPhone page silent). In Calendar, future instances are dimmed and can only be completed after the current one. [Mac guide](https://support.apple.com/gu-in/guide/reminders/mark-reminders-as-completed-remndbeda47c/5.0/mac/10.13), [Use reminders in Calendar](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/use-reminders-iph14f1d32a5/ios)
- Undo: tapping the circle again marks incomplete (Calendar page). Deleted items recoverable for 30 days from Recently Deleted. [Delete and recover](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/delete-and-recover-reminders-iph51b488c05/ios)

### 6. Family / sharing
- Share list via Share button (Messages, Mail, Copy Link, AirDrop). Invitees must accept; all participants can add/edit/complete items and see everyone's changes; all must be signed in to iCloud with Reminders on. "Allow others to invite" toggle controls whether participants can invite. [Share and collaborate](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/share-and-collaborate-iph2a8f9121e/ios), [105124](https://support.apple.com/en-us/105124)
- View-only permission level: silent in iPhone excerpts.
- Assign: on a shared list, tap reminder > Assignment button > person; Reassign / Remove Assignment; multi-select supports Assign. Early Reminder available for assigned tasks (iOS 17+). [105124](https://support.apple.com/en-us/105124)
- Notifications: Collaborate button > Manage Shared List > "Notify When": Adding Items, Completing Items (per-user toggles). Personal notifications set on a reminder are not shared with others. [105124](https://support.apple.com/en-us/105124)
- Activity attribution (who added/completed): silent.
- Family Sharing: joining a Family Sharing group auto-creates a shared list called "Family" visible to all members (documented in Mac guide; iPhone page refers to it). [Mac Family list](https://support.apple.com/en-is/guide/mac-help/mh604eda194e/11.0/mac/11.0)

### 7. Views
- Sort By: due date, creation date, priority, title (not in all Smart Lists); manual order by drag. Show List Info for name/colour/icon. Multi-select toolbar: change date, move, delete, assign, complete, tag, flag. [Edit and organize a list](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/edit-and-organize-a-list-iph82596cb20/ios)
- Columns view of sections (§1). Print: More > Print. [Print a list](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/print-a-list-iph46445b490/ios)
- Widgets: Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets let you complete items; Edit Widget chooses the list. [Widgets](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/add-edit-and-remove-widgets-iphb8f1bf206/ios)
- Calendar: Calendars > Scheduled Reminders shows them; create/complete/edit from Calendar; new ones go to a chosen or default list. [Use reminders in Calendar](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/use-reminders-iph14f1d32a5/ios)

### 8. Offline / sync
- iCloud sync: changes on one device appear on all; troubleshooting article only. Offline behaviour and conflict handling: silent. [iCloud and Reminders](https://support.apple.com/guide/icloud/what-you-can-do-with-icloud-and-reminders-mmc591432bd9/icloud), [102543](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102543)

### 9. Distinctive
- Groceries list type (iOS 17+): items auto-sorted into categories such as Produce, Meat, Frozen Foods, Seafood; Info > Section moves an item; "New Section with Selection" creates a custom category; the list remembers manual regrouping; categorisation only in supported languages (extra language addable in Settings; Apple Intelligence handles mixed-language lists). Convert an existing list via List Info > List Type. [Make a grocery list](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/make-a-grocery-list-iph80ba26e1f/ios), [105086](https://support.apple.com/en-us/105086)
- Mac guide adds: rename/reorder/add categories, "Hide Empty Sections", uncategorised items sit at the bottom. [Mac grocery categories](https://support.apple.com/en-euro/guide/reminders/remnbb50db03/mac)

## Google Tasks (Help Centre + Tasks API)

### 1. Structure
- Multiple lists per account; each user has at least one default list ("My Tasks"). [API overview](https://developers.google.com/tasks/concepts)
- Sections/headers: none documented. Sub-tasks: Task options > Add a subtask (Ctrl/Cmd+] to indent); indented under the parent; tasks with subtasks cannot repeat. [Add or edit a task](https://support.google.com/tasks/answer/7675838?hl=en&co=GENIE.Platform%3DDesktop)
- Groups of lists, templates, pinned lists: silent (none).

### 2. Item fields
- Title, details (notes), date/time, repeat, subtasks, star. [Add or edit a task](https://support.google.com/tasks/answer/7675838?hl=en&co=GENIE.Platform%3DDesktop)
- API Task resource: title, notes, due, status, completed, parent, position, links[], hidden, deleted, assignmentInfo (read-only; source Docs or Chat Space, with driveResourceInfo/spaceInfo). `due` is RFC 3339 but the time portion is discarded; time cannot be read or written via the API. [tasks resource](https://developers.google.com/workspace/tasks/reference/rest/v1/tasks)
- Priority: star only. Tags, flag, attachments, URL field, location reminders: silent (none). Notifications require both a date and a time. [Tasks product page](https://workspace.google.com/products/tasks/)
- Repeat: Repeats every day/week/month/year; Ends Never / On date / After N occurrences; edit next instance only or Stop repeating (cannot re-enable); mobile/Calendar offer "This task / This and following / All tasks"; recurring tasks cannot be moved between lists; shared tasks and subtasks cannot repeat. [Manage repeating tasks](https://support.google.com/tasks/answer/12132599?hl=en&co=GENIE.Platform%3DDesktop)

### 3. Capture
- Add a task in Gmail/Calendar/Docs/Chat side panel or mobile app. Gmail: "Add to Tasks" toolbar button or drag email onto the Tasks panel; mobile Gmail More > Add to Tasks; date/time added afterwards. [Create a task in Gmail](https://support.google.com/mail/answer/9920317?hl=en&co=GENIE.Platform%3DDesktop)
- Natural-language date parsing in the task title: silent.
- Google Assistant: "remind me to …" with a time creates the item in Tasks (and Calendar) since the 2023 migration of Assistant/Calendar reminders into Tasks. [Google blog](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/workspace/tasks-reminders/), [Workspace Updates](https://workspaceupdates.googleblog.com/2023/02/calendar-and-assistant-reminders-to-tasks-migration.html)
- Email-to-task address, share sheet, autocomplete from history: silent.

### 4. Smart lists
- Starred view across lists (sort Date or Recently starred; no manual reorder). [Organize your tasks](https://support.google.com/tasks/answer/7675629?hl=en&co=GENIE.Platform%3DDesktop)
- Today/Scheduled/All/Flagged/Assigned-to-me views and custom smart lists: silent (none). Calendar's "Pending tasks" lists tasks from the last 365 days. [Tasks in Calendar](https://support.google.com/tasks/answer/9901136?hl=en&co=GENIE.Platform%3DDesktop)

### 5. Completion
- Completed tasks drop into a collapsible "Completed" section at the bottom of the list (Down/Up arrow to show/hide). List options > Delete all completed tasks. Undo offered after deleting. [Organize your tasks (Android)](https://support.google.com/tasks/answer/7675629?hl=en&co=GENIE.Platform%3DAndroid), [Delete a task or list](https://support.google.com/tasks/answer/7675690?hl=en&co=GENIE.Platform%3DDesktop)
- API: `tasks.clear` marks completed tasks `hidden` and they stop being returned by default; `completed` timestamp set; completed+hidden tasks cannot be nested. [tasks.move](https://developers.google.com/workspace/tasks/reference/rest/v1/tasks/move)
- Recurring: completing creates the next instance on its date. [Manage repeating tasks](https://support.google.com/tasks/answer/12132599?hl=en&co=GENIE.Platform%3DDesktop)

### 6. Family / sharing
- No shared lists. Collaboration = individual tasks assigned from a Chat space or Docs checklist; they land in the assignee's default list and can be moved/edited/deleted. Notifications when a shared task is created, assigned/reassigned/unassigned, completed or marked incomplete, deleted without completing, or date/title changed; no notifications for your own changes. [Get started with shared tasks](https://support.google.com/tasks/answer/11549608?hl=en), [Assign from Docs](https://support.google.com/tasks/answer/12048749?hl=en&co=GENIE.Platform%3DDesktop), [Chat tasks](https://support.google.com/chat/answer/9920628?hl=en&co=GENIE.Platform%3DDesktop)
- Edit rights, family groups, activity attribution: silent (not a feature).

### 7. Views
- Sort by: My order, Date, Deadline, Starred recently, Title; manual drag only in My order; drag task to another list. [Organize your tasks](https://support.google.com/tasks/answer/7675629?hl=en&co=GENIE.Platform%3DDesktop)
- Print: List options > Print list (Ctrl/Cmd+P); completed printed via Completed section. [Print your tasks](https://support.google.com/tasks/answer/12298851?hl=en)
- Calendar: dated tasks appear when the "Tasks" calendar is ticked; deadline shows in the all-day area; completed tasks shown by default. [Tasks in Calendar](https://support.google.com/tasks/answer/9901136?hl=en&co=GENIE.Platform%3DDesktop)
- Widgets, grouping, column view: silent.

### 8. Offline / sync
- Privacy page: "if you choose to access these files offline, we store this info on your device". Conflict handling: silent. Storage limit 100,000 tasks per account (API 403/429). [Privacy](https://support.google.com/tasks/answer/10431521?hl=en), [API reference](https://developers.google.com/workspace/tasks/reference/rest)

### 9. Distinctive
- Starring as the single priority axis with its own cross-list view. [Workspace Updates 2022](https://workspaceupdates.googleblog.com/2022/06/star-important-tasks-in-google-tasks.html)
- Public REST API with `position`/`parent`/`previous` ordering model and `destinationTasklist` moves. [tasks.move](https://developers.google.com/workspace/tasks/reference/rest/v1/tasks/move)

## Any.do (support.any.do)

### 1. Structure
- Personal Space (private lists) vs Family Space / Workspace (shared boards). Family: up to 4 members, up to 4 shareable boards or grocery lists; personal lists cannot be shared. [Spaces](https://support.any.do/en/articles/9961171-getting-started-with-any-do-spaces-personal-family-and-workspace), [Getting Started with Family](https://support.any.do/en/articles/8761243-getting-started-with-family)
- Boards have sections (e.g. To Do / In Progress / Done), tags and filters; Family boards are Kanban-only. [Sections, tags, filters](https://support.any.do/en/articles/8635235-organizing-boards-with-sections-tags-and-filters), [Board views](https://support.any.do/en/articles/8635279-choosing-the-right-board-view-kanban-table-calendar-gantt)
- Personal lists: create/rename/delete; unlimited on free; sections within personal lists: silent. [Managing Personal Lists](https://support.any.do/en/articles/8635848-managing-personal-lists-in-any-do)
- Sub-tasks (personal) and checklists (boards; each item assignable with its own due date). Templates, groups of lists, pinned lists: silent. [Tasks and Subtasks](https://support.any.do/en/articles/8635837-managing-personal-tasks-and-subtasks), [Assigning](https://support.any.do/en/articles/9571568-assigning-members-to-tasks-checklists)
- Personal lists and tasks can be converted to space items. [Converting](https://support.any.do/en/articles/8637372-converting-personal-items-to-space-items)

### 2. Item fields
- Title (1000 chars), notes, subtasks, attachments (free: 1.5 MB per task), due date (day + time, no notification), reminder (separate; one-time or recurring), colour tags (free: one yellow "Priority" tag; paid: unlimited custom), board tags (multiple per task). [Tasks and Subtasks](https://support.any.do/en/articles/8635837-managing-personal-tasks-and-subtasks), [Premium](https://support.any.do/en/articles/9518709-upgrading-to-any-do-premium), [Color tags](https://support.any.do/en/articles/8635908-using-color-tags-for-personal-tasks), [Glossary](https://support.any.do/en/articles/9784438-the-any-do-glossary)
- Repeat: basic (daily/weekly/monthly/yearly) on free; advanced (custom intervals, end dates, day-of-week rules) on paid; set from task view not quick add. Location reminders (arrive/leave) paid, mobile only; a task has either a time or a location reminder. [Personal Reminders](https://support.any.do/en/articles/8635373-personal-reminders-one-time-and-recurring), [Notifications overview](https://support.any.do/en/articles/12812921-notifications-reminders-overview)
- Board due dates with reminder offsets (on time, minutes/hours/day before). [Board due dates](https://support.any.do/en/articles/8635318-board-due-dates-reminders-and-recurring-tasks)
- Assignee: one or more board members per task; checklist items assignable separately. [Assigning](https://support.any.do/en/articles/9571568-assigning-members-to-tasks-checklists)
- URL field, flag, "when messaging": silent.

### 3. Capture
- Quick Add bar with Smart Type: parses "Call Rene tomorrow", "Pay rent next week", "Submit report Friday at 9am", "Dentist in 2 hours", "Meeting on January 12"; `#tag` adds/creates tags; list and reminder set via icons, not parsed; mobile Voice Input; AI Voice Mode ("Add call John … to my Work list"). [Quick Add](https://support.any.do/en/articles/8637042-quick-add-bar-smart-type-and-voice-input-in-any-do), [Voice Mode](https://support.any.do/en/articles/14775167-any-do-voice-mode-capture-tasks-instantly-with-ai)
- Siri: enable in Settings; "Siri, remind me to …" syncs via Apple Reminders list; "add to Any.do" phrasing does not work. Google Assistant/Alexa: silent. [Siri](https://support.any.do/en/articles/8634346-any-do-siri-apple-reminders)
- Email-to-task: send from the account email; subject = title, body = notes; plain text only. WhatsApp task creation (paid). Browser extensions (Chrome/Safari/Firefox/Edge). [Emailing tasks](https://support.any.do/en/articles/8636554-emailing-tasks-to-any-do), [WhatsApp](https://support.any.do/whatsapp-reminders)
- Share sheet, autocomplete from history: silent in support docs (vendor blog claims a grocery list that "learns": [blog](https://blog.any.do/how-a-shared-grocery-list-that-learns-saves-you-time-every-week/)).

### 4. Smart lists
- Fixed views: My Day, Next 7 Days, All Tasks, Calendar. Only tasks assigned to you appear in 7 Days / All Tasks / Calendar; grocery items appear in none of them. Custom smart lists: silent (Workspace has custom views/reports). [Navigation](https://support.any.do/en/articles/8636803-getting-around-in-any-do-app-navigation-and-main-views), [Assigning](https://support.any.do/en/articles/9571568-assigning-members-to-tasks-checklists), [Grocery](https://support.any.do/en/articles/8635928-using-smart-grocery-lists-in-any-do)
- Board filters: assignee, status, tags, sections, due dates, combinable. [Sections, tags, filters](https://support.any.do/en/articles/8635235-organizing-boards-with-sections-tags-and-filters)

### 5. Completion
- Completed personal tasks stay visible (struck through) in the list until archived or deleted; cannot be edited until reactivated; reactivate by tapping the circle again (web: "Completed" > select). Archive moves items to an archive folder; archived board items restorable (web only). [Completing & Archiving](https://support.any.do/en/articles/8635866-completing-archiving-personal-tasks), [Board archive](https://support.any.do/en/articles/8635255-completing-and-archiving-board-tasks)
- Recurring: next occurrence created only after the current one is marked done; completing several in one day can skip occurrences. [Personal Reminders](https://support.any.do/en/articles/8635373-personal-reminders-one-time-and-recurring)
- Overdue: shown under Today; greyed in calendar for drag-rescheduling; Moment reschedules them in bulk. [Overdue](https://support.any.do/en/articles/8635857-managing-overdue-personal-tasks)

### 6. Family / sharing
- Family Space created by a Space Admin; only admins invite members; adding to the Space does not add to boards; board roles decide view/comment/manage. [Create a space](https://support.any.do/en/articles/8610805-how-to-create-a-family-space-workspace), [Roles](https://support.any.do/en/articles/8614000-roles-permissions-space-vs-board)
- Share a board: Share button or + beside member avatars. [Board members](https://support.any.do/en/articles/8635268-adding-members-to-boards)
- Notifications: push (Android/iOS, snooze/complete actions), desktop, Notifications Center for mentions/assignments/status changes; per-user per-board email triggers: task created, moved to section, tagged, archived (completion/member-added not listed). Personal reminders never notify other members. [Notifications overview](https://support.any.do/en/articles/12812921-notifications-reminders-overview), [Email alerts](https://support.any.do/en/articles/8616775-notifications-center-and-email-alerts-for-boards)
- Attribution: board Activity section logs all updates, exportable, web only; task chat with @name/@Assignees/@Everyone. [Activity](https://support.any.do/en/articles/8613867-reviewing-board-activity-history), [Assigning](https://support.any.do/en/articles/9571568-assigning-members-to-tasks-checklists)
- Shared calendar: each member connects the same Google/Outlook calendar; no native family calendar. [Getting Started with Family](https://support.any.do/en/articles/8761243-getting-started-with-family)

### 7. Views
- Board views Kanban/Table/Calendar/Gantt (Family: Kanban only). Sort options for personal lists: silent beyond "advanced sorting" on Premium. [Board views](https://support.any.do/en/articles/8635279-choosing-the-right-board-view-kanban-table-calendar-gantt), [Managing Personal Lists](https://support.any.do/en/articles/8635848-managing-personal-lists-in-any-do)
- Print/export: lists, All Tasks, 7 Days, Archived, boards; not the calendar view. [Printing](https://support.any.do/en/articles/8635961-printing-and-exporting-items)
- Widgets: Android 4 widgets + notification Quick Add bar; iOS 11 Home Screen widgets, no Lock Screen widgets. [Widgets](https://support.any.do/en/articles/8637157-the-any-do-widgets)
- Calendar integration: Google/Outlook/iCloud (web/desktop one account; mobile mirrors device calendars); events read from provider, refresh schedule varies. [Calendar integration](https://support.any.do/en/articles/8610622-how-to-set-up-the-calendar-integration)

### 8. Offline / sync
- Data syncs to servers continuously; offline use "possible in limited scenarios, not recommended for long periods"; no full local database, so changes may not save until reconnected. Conflicts: silent. [Sync and back up](https://support.any.do/en/articles/8610074-how-to-sync-and-back-up-your-any-do-account)

### 9. Distinctive
- Smart Grocery Lists: items auto-sorted into aisle categories (Dairy, Produce, Bakery…); "Change category" on mobile, "Move category" on web (family only); no custom categories; personal grocery lists are mobile-only and sync to web without categories; grocery items are not standard tasks (absent from All Tasks/Calendar/My Day). [Grocery](https://support.any.do/en/articles/8635928-using-smart-grocery-lists-in-any-do)
- My Day: daily plan you populate yourself; Smart Suggestions include yesterday's unfinished tasks; Moment: guided review/reschedule of overdue items (free: once a day). Daily push at 9 AM by default prompts planning.
