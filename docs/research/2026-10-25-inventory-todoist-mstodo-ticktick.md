# Todo-app feature research: Todoist, Microsoft To Do, TickTick

Researched 2026-10-25 from vendor-owned sources only (todoist.com help centre and developer docs, support.microsoft.com and learn.microsoft.com, help.ticktick.com and developer.ticktick.com).

**Method note.** This session's network policy denied direct HTTPS to every vendor host (403 on CONNECT for todoist.com, developer.todoist.com, support.microsoft.com, learn.microsoft.com, help.ticktick.com, developer.ticktick.com). Every claim below was therefore gathered today via domain-restricted web search over those vendor pages (titles, URLs and page excerpts), not by rendering the pages. Where a search did not surface a statement, the item is marked **silent**. Community Q&A on learn.microsoft.com/answers is not treated as vendor documentation and is flagged where used. Some help articles are several years old; the vendor help centre is the source of truth.

Legend: **silent** = not stated in the vendor docs surfaced; (community) = user forum, not official.

## Todoist

### 1. Structure
- Projects hold tasks; the **Inbox** is the default project. Sub-projects (nested projects) exist only in personal "My Projects"; team workspaces use folders instead. Archiving removes a project from the list with view/restore later; unarchive via the archived-projects filter under My Projects. [Introduction to projects](https://www.todoist.com/help/articles/introduction-to-projects-TLTjNftLM)
- **Sections** split a project; archived sections are hidden until "Completed tasks" is turned on and appear greyed out. Limit: 20 sections per project. [Introduction to sections](https://www.todoist.com/help/articles/introduction-to-sections-rOrK0aEn), [Usage limits](https://www.todoist.com/help/account-and-billing/plans/usage-limits-in-todoist-e5rcSY)
- **Sub-tasks**: four indent levels (sub-task = level 1, sub-sub-sub-sub-task = level 4). Parent and child must be in the same project; sorting must be off to drag-indent. Completed sub-tasks stay visible crossed out until the parent is completed. [Use sub-tasks](https://www.todoist.com/help/articles/introduction-to-sub-tasks-kMamDo)
- **Favourites**: projects, labels and filters can be favourited; they appear in a Favorites sidebar section and sync across devices. [Favorites](https://www.todoist.com/help/todoist/features/add-a-project-label-or-filter-to-favorites-in-todoist-zezDfvSK)
- Limits: 300 active tasks per project (all plans, cannot be raised); 5 active personal projects on Beginner (free), 300 on Pro; 500 labels per account, 100 labels per task, 60-char label names; 100 templates. [Usage limits](https://www.todoist.com/help/account-and-billing/plans/usage-limits-in-todoist-e5rcSY)

### 2. Item fields
- Title; **description** (formatted text, links, attachments; 16,383 chars). [Task view](https://www.todoist.com/help/todoist/features/use-the-task-view-to-manage-tasks-in-todoist-eDeRDO0C)
- **Date** (when you plan to work) vs **deadline** (last day it can be finished; paid plans only; quick-add syntax `{march 30}`). Date can carry a time and a duration (default 30 min when placed on calendar). [Deadlines](https://www.todoist.com/help/todoist/features/introduction-to-deadlines-in-todoist-uMqbSLM6U), [Time blocking](https://www.todoist.com/help/todoist/get-started/time-blocking-in-todoist-d6Pf1uTpc)
- **Reminders**: automatic reminders for tasks with date+time are free; custom time-based and **location-based** (arrive/leave, mobile only) need Pro/Business. Quick-add syntax `!14:00`, `!30 min before`, `!every 5pm`, `!every sat 9am`. [Reminders](https://www.todoist.com/help/articles/introduction-to-reminders-9PezfU)
- **Recurring grammar**: `every day`, `every week`, `every other week`, `every mon, fri at 20:00`, `every 12 hours starting at 9pm`, `every 3rd Tuesday starting Aug 29 ending in 6 months`, `everyday from 10 May until 20 May`, `every! 3 months`. Plain `every` computes the next date from the scheduled date; `every!` computes from completion date. One `every` per task; several days and hours cannot be combined in one rule. A visual Repeat picker exists as an alternative. [Recurring dates](https://www.todoist.com/help/articles/introduction-to-recurring-due-dates-YUYVJJAV), [Visual recurring interface](https://www.todoist.com/help/articles/new-visual-interface-for-recurring-tasks-jun-30-nMZ4CZjHb)
- **Priority**: four levels p1 (highest) to p4 (default); names/colours not customisable. [Set a priority](https://www.todoist.com/help/articles/set-a-priority-in-todoist-Wy82Jp)
- **Labels**: cross-project tags; `%label` in quick add (`@` still works, planned to retire end of 2026). [Labels](https://www.todoist.com/help/articles/introduction-to-labels-dSo2eE)
- **Comments and files**: speech-bubble opens comments; paperclip attaches files; comments are chronological and cannot be reordered. Uploads 5 MB free / 25 MB Pro. [Comments and files](https://todoist.com/help/articles/introduction-to-comments-and-file-uploads-CwiA50), [Usage limits](https://www.todoist.com/help/account-and-billing/plans/usage-limits-in-todoist-e5rcSY)
- **Assignee**: one person per task, only in shared projects (`+name` in quick add after `#project`). [Quick Add](https://www.todoist.com/help/articles/use-task-quick-add-in-todoist-va4Lhpzz)

### 3. Capture and smart lists
- **Quick Add** tokens: natural date, `#project`, `/section` (after `#`), `%label`, `+assignee`, `p1`-`p4`, `!reminder`, `{deadline}`. Inline description token: **silent** (docs say tap + under the name). Mis-parsed words (e.g. "monthly" in "Create monthly report") can be clicked to revert to plain text. [Quick Add](https://www.todoist.com/help/articles/use-task-quick-add-in-todoist-va4Lhpzz), [Customize Quick Add](https://www.todoist.com/help/articles/customize-quick-add-in-todoist-eqRRlZJNN)
- **Date parsing examples**: `tomorrow at 4pm`, `tom morning` (= 9am next day), `in 3 weeks`, `3rd friday jan`, `next tuesday` (relative to current week: on a Monday it skips this week's Tuesday). Not parsed in Czech/Turkish. [Dates and time](https://www.todoist.com/help/articles/introduction-to-dates-and-time-q7VobO)
- **Email-to-task**: each project has a unique address; subject becomes task name, body becomes a comment attachment; dates/`@label`/`p1` in subject are parsed. Forwarding to a task's address adds a comment. [Forward emails](https://todoist.com/help/articles/forward-emails-to-todoist-JPJ1V339)
- **Siri**: "Add [task] in Todoist"; say "<project> list" to target a project; no project goes to Inbox. **Shortcuts** "Create Task" action usable from the share sheet and widgets. **Share extension** on iOS/Android lets you pick project, due date, priority. **Ramble** dictation (10 sessions/month free). [Siri](https://www.todoist.com/help/articles/use-siri-with-todoist-zDdrA4), [Shortcuts](https://www.todoist.com/help/articles/use-shortcuts-with-todoist-for-ios-xGxBVSMr), [Ramble](https://www.todoist.com/help/articles/dictate-to-add-tasks-with-ramble-P1Raq7vVF)
- **Smart views**: Inbox, Today, Upcoming (up to two years ahead). "Assigned to me" is a filter query (`assigned to: me`) or a Display > Assignee > Only me option, not a built-in view. No "Flagged" view; priority filters (`p1`) serve that role. [Upcoming](https://www.todoist.com/help/articles/plan-your-week-with-the-upcoming-view-OKOg1mR8), [Manage team tasks](https://www.todoist.com/help/articles/manage-team-tasks-in-todoist-S99543QzY)

### 4. Completion
- Completed tasks are hidden by default; toggle "Completed tasks" in a project's/Today's/Upcoming's Display settings to show them and click the circle to uncomplete. Reporting (formerly activity log) lists completed tasks across projects but cannot uncomplete them. [View completed](https://www.todoist.com/help/todoist/features/view-completed-tasks-in-todoist-J19h2s)
- **Recurring completion**: completing moves the task to its next date and leaves the finished occurrence as a record; a completed recurring occurrence cannot be uncompleted. An **Undo** toast appears bottom-left for a few seconds and shifts the date back. Menu options: Complete, Complete & reset sub-tasks, **Complete forever** (ends recurrence). [Complete a recurring task](https://www.todoist.com/help/todoist/features/complete-a-task-with-a-recurring-date-dmI6SVqdP)
- **Reset sub-tasks**: re-adds up to 30 sub-tasks on each recurrence; ignores sub-sub-tasks; order not preserved. [Reset a sub-task](https://www.todoist.com/help/articles/reset-a-sub-task-in-todoist-V6SRBGPw0)
- Overdue: a **Reschedule** button in Today/Upcoming moves all overdue tasks at once (only visible when grouped by date); dragging to the bottom of Today postpones to tomorrow; overdue tasks cannot be manually sorted. [Today view](https://www.todoist.com/help/todoist/get-started/plan-your-day-with-the-today-view-UVUXaiSs), [Default sorting](https://www.todoist.com/help/todoist/features/default-sorting-order-in-todoist-mqmgerY7)
- "Clear completed": **silent** (completed tasks are simply hidden).

### 5. Family and sharing
- Share a project via invite link or by name/email; invitees get Can Edit. Personal projects: up to 5 collaborators; team projects: 250. Team projects alone have roles/permissions and a team activity view. [Collaborate with friends or family](https://www.todoist.com/help/articles/collaborate-with-friends-or-family-in-todoist-tzkGUy), [Work with others](https://www.todoist.com/help/articles/work-with-others-in-todoist-WOpFVjup7)
- Assignment: single assignee per task in shared projects. Activity log/Reporting is filterable by project, person ("By collaborator") and event type. [Reporting](https://www.todoist.com/help/articles/view-the-activity-log-in-todoist-oOra6D)
- Notifications: per-event settings for shared projects (e.g. "Tasks assigned to you" by email, push or both); one desktop/web toggle. [Notifications](https://www.todoist.com/help/articles/manage-your-notifications-in-todoist-QxQGXkMu)
- Personal planning view: Today (smart-sorted; manual sort optional) and Upcoming; no separate "My Day" concept.

### 6. Views
- Five layouts: list, **board** (kanban; columns = sections; works on Today/Upcoming/labels/filters; print falls back to list), **calendar** day/week/month (Pro/Business; tasks coloured by priority, label or project). [Board layout](https://www.todoist.com/help/articles/use-the-board-layout-in-todoist-AiAVsyEI), [Calendar layout](https://www.todoist.com/help/todoist/features/use-the-calendar-layout-in-todoist-lPHRQTu0o), [Customize views](https://www.todoist.com/help/articles/customize-views-in-todoist-AoHhBxFdZ)
- Sort: Manual, Name, Date, Date added, Deadline, Priority. Group by: date, date added, deadline, priority, label. Display options are per-user unless "Save for everyone". [Sort or group](https://www.todoist.com/help/todoist/features/sort-or-group-tasks-in-todoist-WFWD0hrb)
- **Filters** (saved queries): `&`, `|`, `!`, wildcards (`%urgent*`), `search: Meeting & today`, `overdue & !no time`, `assigned to: me`, `workspace: name`, `#project`, `p1`. 3 filters free / 150 Pro. [Filters](https://www.todoist.com/help/articles/introduction-to-filters-V98wIH), [Search](https://www.todoist.com/help/articles/introduction-to-search-fAfiDSAp)
- Widgets (Android): Add task shortcut, Task list (choose view/project/label/filter), Productivity (Karma), Ramble. iOS widgets: **silent** in surfaced docs. [Android widget](https://www.todoist.com/help/todoist/features/use-a-todoist-widget-on-your-android-device-632pZA)

### 7. Offline and sync
- Offline mode activates automatically; add/edit/delete work offline and sync on reconnect. Closing the app, logging out or clearing local data before sync completes deletes the unsynced changes; a "Discard unsynced changes?" prompt exists. Web offline data lives in browser storage. Conflict resolution between two devices: **silent**. [Use Todoist offline](https://www.todoist.com/help/todoist/features/use-todoist-while-offline-4rbaZw)
- Changelog: a task added offline to a project deleted before sync now lands in Inbox with a note. [2026 changelog](https://www.todoist.com/help/articles/2026-changelog-HD3jJAtLd)
- **Sync API idempotency**: each command carries a client-generated `uuid`; "Todoist will not execute a command that has same UUID as a previously executed command", so clients may safely retry. `temp_id` is a client ID for created resources, returned in `temp_id_mapping`; `sync_status` is keyed by command uuid. Incremental sync via `sync_token` (`*` for full). [Todoist API v1](https://developer.todoist.com/api/v1/)

### 8. Distinctive
- **Karma**: points for completing tasks on time, using labels/recurring dates/reminders, hitting daily/weekly goals; eight levels Beginner (0-499) to Enlightened (50,000+); points lost for tasks 5+ days overdue; streaks; vacation mode protects streaks. [Karma](https://www.todoist.com/help/todoist/features/introduction-to-karma-OgWkWy), [Productivity view](https://www.todoist.com/help/todoist/features/use-the-productivity-view-in-todoist-6S63uAa9)
- **Templates**: save a project as a template (colour, description, sections and section descriptions kept); gallery with My templates; CSV import/export; templates do not stay synced with the source project. [Templates](https://www.todoist.com/help/todoist/features/introduction-to-templates-in-todoist-uofJ8i40M), [CSV](https://www.todoist.com/help/articles/import-or-export-a-project-as-a-csv-file-in-todoist-YC8YvN)
- Start dates: not supported as a field (docs explain how to approximate). [Start dates](https://www.todoist.com/help/articles/does-todoist-support-start-dates-qhqlgZhk)

## Microsoft To Do

### 1. Structure
- Lists; **list groups** (create via the group icon next to "+ New List", drag lists in). No sections inside a list. [Welcome to Microsoft To Do](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/todo/welcome-to-microsoft-to-do)
- **Steps** are one-level sub-items; the parent shows "n of m steps completed". Steps have no own due date (community). Nesting depth: one level (steps cannot have steps). [What's new](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/office/what-s-new-in-microsoft-to-do-e4385509-13af-4a55-ae17-1c5a61cf2d41), [Step due dates (community)](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/answers/questions/5628944/is-it-possible-to-set-different-due-dates-for-the)
- Favourites/archive of lists: **silent**. Graph API models the hierarchy as `todoTaskList` > `todoTask` > `checklistItem`. [todoTaskList](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/graph/api/resources/todotasklist?view=graph-rest-1.0)

### 2. Item fields
- Title; **note** (add a note or file in the detail view); **Add a file** up to 25 MB per task, files only in personal lists (community). [Add files](https://support.microsoft.com/en-au/todo/add-files-to-your-tasks), [Manage tasks with To Do in Outlook](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/office/manage-tasks-with-to-do-in-outlook-6e8a991b-ea62-4009-a7f7-62b70a57ec18)
- **Due date** presets Today / Tomorrow / Next week / pick a date; **Remind me** presets Later today / Tomorrow / Next week / pick date and time. Reminders are per user in shared lists (community). Location reminders: **silent**. [Due dates and reminders](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/todo/add-due-dates-and-reminders-in-microsoft-to-do)
- **Repeat**: Daily, Weekdays, Weekly, Monthly, Yearly, Custom. API grammar is `patternedRecurrence` = `recurrencePattern` (type daily/weekly/absoluteMonthly/relativeMonthly/absoluteYearly/relativeYearly, `interval`, `daysOfWeek`, `dayOfMonth`, `index` first..last, `firstDayOfWeek`) + `recurrenceRange` (type endDate/noEnd/numbered). No "repeat from completion" mode documented. [Due dates and reminders](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/todo/add-due-dates-and-reminders-in-microsoft-to-do), [recurrencePattern](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/graph/api/resources/recurrencepattern?view=graph-rest-1.0), [recurrenceRange](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/graph/api/resources/recurrencerange?view=graph-rest-1.0)
- **Priority**: single boolean "Mark task as important" (star). [Manage tasks with To Do in Outlook](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/office/manage-tasks-with-to-do-in-outlook-6e8a991b-ea62-4009-a7f7-62b70a57ec18)
- **Tags**: `#hashtag` typed in the title; found via search only. [Manage tasks with To Do in Outlook](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/outlook/calendar/manage-tasks-with-to-do-in-outlook)
- Comments: **silent** (none documented). **Assignee**: one member of a shared list via "Assign to". [Assign tasks in shared lists](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/todo/assign-tasks-in-shared-lists)

### 3. Capture and smart lists
- **Smart date recognition** in the title: typing "tomorrow", "daily", "every Monday" sets due date/repeat on save; toggle in Settings; not applied when editing in the detail view; can misfire on names. [Smart due date recognition](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/office/smart-due-date-reminder-recognition-in-microsoft-to-do-b8cde14b-28da-42e1-9ce8-d75cc3717993)
- **Flagged Email** smart list turns Outlook flagged mail into tasks (optional). Email-to-task address: **silent**. [My Day in Outlook](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/outlook/create-and-manage-task-lists-with-my-day-in-outlook)
- Siri: official page covers only iOS 9/10; iOS app offers "Add Task" and "Open List" Siri Shortcuts (community). Share sheet: **silent**. [Siri iOS 9/10](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/office/using-siri-with-microsoft-to-do-on-ios-9-and-ios-10-9a8e7246-8c77-448a-886f-59fd8e0afbaa)
- **Smart lists**: My Day, Important, Planned (tasks with due date or reminder; cannot add directly), All, Completed, Assigned to me, Flagged Email, Due Today; each can be turned off in Settings > Smart lists. [My Day in Outlook](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/outlook/create-and-manage-task-lists-with-my-day-in-outlook), [Assign tasks](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/todo/assign-tasks-in-shared-lists)

### 4. Completion
- Completed tasks stay in the list under a collapsible **Completed** heading; List options has "Hide completed tasks"/"Show completed tasks"; a Completed smart list aggregates them; uncomplete by toggling the checkbox in the Completed list. [Screen reader: lists](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/accessibility/todo/use-a-screen-reader-to-work-with-lists-in-to-do), [Screen reader: tasks](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/accessibility/todo/use-a-screen-reader-to-work-with-tasks-in-to-do)
- Recurring: completing creates the next occurrence on the schedule (fixed-schedule, not completion-based); no built-in "complete forever" or occurrence cap (community); reminder on the next instance may not shift (community). [Recurring task re-appearing (community)](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/answers/questions/5278274/microsoft-to-do-how-do-you-stop-recurring-tasks-fr)
- Undo and "clear completed": **silent**.

### 5. Family and sharing
- Sharing is per list: Share list > Create invitation link; works between personal Microsoft accounts or within one work/school tenant; only Microsoft-account holders can join. Owner can stop sharing or "Limit access to current members". [Create and share lists](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/todo/create-and-share-lists)
- Assign to any member; moving a task to another list removes the assignment; assignee sees it in "Assigned to me". [Assign tasks in shared lists](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/todo/assign-tasks-in-shared-lists)
- Activity log / change notifications for others' edits: **silent**.
- **My Day**: a daily personal list that resets every night; unfinished items return to their lists and reappear as **Suggestions** (due today, overdue, previously in My Day, or "relevant"). Daily-repeating tasks are suggested each morning. [My Day and suggestions](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/office/my-day-and-suggestions-fc09a1b9-0854-4906-b166-f480ee97a139), [Daily habits](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/office/creating-daily-habits-with-microsoft-to-do-cf2a37ea-8f7b-450c-a401-b131365728b1)

### 6. Views
- List view only; no board/kanban, no calendar view. Sort by importance documented (others: **silent**). No grouping, no saved filters; search covers titles/hashtags. [Manage tasks with To Do in Outlook](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/outlook/calendar/manage-tasks-with-to-do-in-outlook)
- Widgets: Android widget (add tasks to My Day, view tasks; app must not be on SD card); iOS 14 widgets (view list, add task, open list; cannot complete tasks from widget). [Android widget](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/office/using-the-android-widget-with-microsoft-to-do-11392322-b3b9-451f-84ba-a70f540909f2), [iOS widgets](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/todo/ios-widgets-and-microsoft-to-do)

### 7. Offline and sync
- Tasks are stored in Exchange Online and sync to Outlook Tasks. Apps work in offline mode; logging out discards unsynced data. [Syncing your account](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/office/syncing-your-microsoft-to-do-account-61aeee82-6b3e-451b-ba80-79e1b66aca33), [Important updates](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/office/important-updates-are-available-for-microsoft-to-do-apps-e5cdff3d-caba-4018-a089-774132900646)
- **Graph API**: `GET /me/todo/lists/{id}/tasks/delta` for incremental sync with `@odata.deltaLink`; token lifetime not fixed. Idempotency keys: **silent** (none). `linkedResources` carry a partner `externalId` + deep link. [todoTask delta](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/graph/api/todotask-delta?view=graph-rest-1.0), [To Do API overview](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/graph/api/resources/todo-overview?view=graph-rest-1.0)

### 8. Distinctive
- My Day + Suggestions as the daily planning ritual; Planner tasks surface in "Assigned to me" when connected; Flagged Email integration. [See Planner tasks](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/planner/see-planner-tasks-in-microsoft-to-do)

## TickTick

### 1. Structure
- Hierarchy: **folder > list > section > task > subtask**. Sections managed via list "..." > Manage Section. Inbox cannot be hidden or deleted. [FAQ](https://help.ticktick.com/articles/7055792921664028672), [Manage Tasks with Lists](https://help.ticktick.com/articles/7055782283059396608)
- **Subtasks** nest up to **5 levels**; each nested task has its own tags, priority and reminders. Older "Check Items" (checklist inside the description) remain as a separate mode toggled in the detail view. Subtasks cannot be created inside smart lists. [Multilevel Tasks](https://help.ticktick.com/articles/7055782219767349248), [Task Details](https://help.ticktick.com/articles/7055782408586526720)
- List archive/close and favourites: **silent** in surfaced docs (archive is documented only for habits and countdowns). [Countdowns](https://help.ticktick.com/articles/7322534343065206784)

### 2. Item fields
- Title; description or check items (toggle top-right of detail view); `/` in the body inserts headings, lists, attachments, subtasks. [Add Tasks](https://help.ticktick.com/articles/7055782422935240704)
- Due date and time; **reminders** incl. **location reminders** (arrive at place; iOS/Android only). [Effective Reminder Feature](https://help.ticktick.com/articles/7055782395743567872), [FAQ](https://help.ticktick.com/articles/7055792921664028672)
- **Repeat**: presets plus Custom with three types: **By Due Dates**, **By Completion Date** (next occurrence computed from completion time), **By Specific Dates**. End via "Repeat Ends On" date or "Repeat Ends After n times". Skip one occurrence (swipe left / right-click). Edit a single occurrence from calendar with "Only this Recurrence". Future occurrences cannot be edited until the current one is completed. Open API stores this as `repeatFlag` (RFC 5545 RRULE string, e.g. `RRULE:FREQ=DAILY;INTERVAL=1`) per community wrappers; official schema not surfaced. [Set Up Recurring Tasks](https://help.ticktick.com/articles/7055782206349770752), [Open API](https://developer.ticktick.com/docs/openapi.md)
- **Priority**: High, Medium, Low, None (URL scheme values 5/3/1/0). [Use Siri & URL Scheme](https://help.ticktick.com/articles/7055781515422072832)
- **Tags**: `#tag` in the input creates a tag; tags have their own views. [Manage Tasks with Tags](https://help.ticktick.com/articles/7055782255804809216)
- **Attachments**: limits differ between pages (20 MB premium / 10 MB free per file; per-task and per-day caps vary). **Comments** on tasks in shared lists (usable as notes in private lists). Task activity log via "..." on the task. [Task Details](https://help.ticktick.com/articles/7055782408586526720)
- **Assignee**: one performer per task in shared lists; bulk assign from the task list. [How Teams Can Collaborate](https://help.ticktick.com/articles/7055781688281923584)

### 3. Capture and smart lists
- **Smart Recognition**: date/time in the title sets due date and reminder ("Meeting at 9 tomorrow"); ambiguous time resolves to the nearest future moment ("remind me at 9pm" typed at 4pm = today 9pm); setting to strip parsed text from the title; press Delete or click the highlight to cancel parsing; toggle under Settings > General > Smart Recognition. Full phrase list: **silent**. [Smart Recognition](https://help.ticktick.com/articles/7081924556310446080)
- Quick add also sets priority, tag, list and date; symbols for list/priority (`~`, `!`) are **silent** in docs (only `#` for tags is documented). Dropdown beside the input: priority, attachments, templates. [Add Tasks](https://help.ticktick.com/articles/7055782422935240704)
- **Email-to-task**: generic address (only from your registered email) or personal address (from any sender); enable in Settings > Integrations & Import. [Add Tasks](https://help.ticktick.com/articles/7055782422935240704)

## Cross-app observations for a family todo + shopping list
- Recurrence has two semantics everywhere worth copying: fixed schedule vs from-completion (Todoist `every!`, TickTick "By Completion Date"); Microsoft offers only fixed. RFC 5545 RRULE (TickTick) or the Graph `patternedRecurrence` shape are the two machine formats seen.
- All three parse dates typed in the title and let the user click the highlight to undo parsing; all three hide completed items under a per-list toggle rather than deleting them.
- Sharing is per list/project with a single assignee per task; only TickTick documents per-member permissions and per-list change notifications; only Microsoft documents a nightly-reset personal "My Day".
- Idempotent writes are documented only by Todoist (command `uuid`); incremental sync is documented by Todoist (`sync_token`) and Microsoft (delta query).
